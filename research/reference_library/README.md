# Research Reference Library (published work only)

This directory is a curated study resource. It is **not** a record of experiments performed by QuantumML Lab.

## Source labels
- `PUBLISHED_REFERENCE`: claims and findings reported by the cited publication.
- `ORIGINAL_EXPERIMENT`: measurements generated by this project's own code (stored separately under `research/original_experiments/`).
- `REPRODUCED_REFERENCE_RESULT`: use only after a documented reproduction run has been completed and checked against the paper's stated conditions.

## Core reading list

### 1. McClean et al. (2018)
**Barren plateaus in quantum neural network training landscapes.** *Nature Communications* 9, 4812 (2018). DOI: [10.1038/s41467-018-07090-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-07090-4). Publisher: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-07090-4

Study focus: establishes a barren-plateau result for certain randomly initialized parameterized quantum circuit settings. The central implication is that gradient variance can become exponentially suppressed with system size under the paper's assumptions. Do not generalize this to every circuit, cost function, or initialization.

### 2. Cerezo et al. (2021)
**Cost function dependent barren plateaus in shallow parametrized quantum circuits.** *Nature Communications* 12, 1791 (2021). DOI: [10.1038/s41467-021-21728-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-21728-w). Publisher: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21728-w

Study focus: shows that barren plateaus depend on cost-function locality under specified ansatz assumptions. The paper contrasts global and local observables and demonstrates that shallow depth alone does not guarantee trainability.

### 3. Ragone et al. (2024)
**A Lie algebraic theory of barren plateaus for deep parameterized quantum circuits.** *Nature Communications* 15, 7172 (2024). DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-49909-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49909-3). Publisher: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-49909-3

Study focus: develops a Lie-algebraic framework for understanding gradient variance in sufficiently deep parameterized circuits, including certain noise settings. Useful for understanding why circuit expressivity and algebraic structure matter.

### 4. Cunningham & Zhuang (2025)
**Investigating and mitigating barren plateaus in variational quantum circuits: a survey.** *Quantum Information Processing* 24, 48 (2025). DOI: [10.1007/s11128-025-04665-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-025-04665-1). Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11128-025-04665-1

Study focus: review of barren-plateau causes and mitigation directions. Use as a map of the literature, then consult the primary papers for detailed claims.

## Research integrity rules
1. Read the paper before using a specific quantitative result.
2. Do not copy numbers from abstracts or secondary summaries as though they were full experimental datasets.
3. Store exact published values only with a page, figure, table, equation, or section locator.
4. Mark unreported values as `Not reported`; never estimate them.
5. A literature summary is a study aid, not a substitute for citing the original work.
6. Keep all project-generated data outside this directory.
